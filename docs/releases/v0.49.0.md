# Kagi 0.49.0 — Settings, rebuilt

Released: 2026-11-06

## Find preferences in eight sidebar panes

Settings now organizes every existing preference into **Appearance, Graph, Editor, Terminal, Network & Sync, Smart Commit, Analyze and General**. The native window keeps a consistent 900×760-point frame, reduced only when needed to fit the visible screen, while longer forms scroll inside their pane. Analyze drafts remain available when changing panes or reopening Settings.

Browse themes in separate **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode** strips. Apple, Catppuccin, Nagi and One families appear in the same order in both appearances; one global selection and keyboard order keep navigation predictable. Theme cards show the actual palette, reveal the selected or focused theme when needed, and preserve independent manual browsing.

Selected sidebar rows and enabled controls remain recognizable while hovered. Keyboard focus stays separate from selection and ON state, with contrast-adjusted rings, neutral OFF switches and controls sized for their labels. English and Japanese labels and feedback wrap within their owning controls rather than being clipped.

## Read complete values and help without hovering

Provider and model choices use keyboard-operable popup menus. Short values retain compact controls; long values wrap visibly within their trigger and menu item. Theme names and sidebar destinations also remain complete, and paired themes share their family order without changing saved theme identities. Monokai now has a concise display name.

Descriptions stay visible in the form instead of being repeated in hover tooltips. The full existing guidance—including Ollama's localhost scope, system Reduce Motion behavior and terminal-lock exceptions—remains available without hovering. Analyze's ignore placeholder and save/reset feedback are localized into Japanese, and the multiline editor retains its actual editing behavior.

## Keep notifications together in their owning surface

Persistent, transient and busy feedback share one bounded **bottom-left notification stack in the content area**, including Conflict Mode and Settings. The stack does not move content or cover the sidebar, Inspector, toolbar or status bar. Busy actions stay reachable while notifications expand, and one scroll viewport preserves access to older errors and their Operation Log detail. Ownership, scoped Escape handling, actions and notification lifetimes remain intact.

## Known limitations

- **Binary, image, PDF and symlink conflict Save stages the chosen side but leaves the working-tree file unchanged**; submodule choices likewise stage the selected gitlink rather than updating its checkout. After Continue/commit, the working tree can still hold the unchosen side as an unstaged change; staging it (for example with **Stage all**) reverts the resolution. This remains tracked in [#1810](https://github.com/TomiXRM/kagi/issues/1810).
- Background draft queue acceptance is **not a durability acknowledgment**. Abrupt exit, slow or failing storage, or shutdown beyond the available flush budget can still lose an unacknowledged edit. Preserved incompatible or colliding drafts may be inspection-only rather than automatically reapplied.
- PDF comparison displays both documents; it does **not** compute or highlight their differences. Complex uncached pages can still require initial rendering time. Password entry and universal font fidelity are not supported; malformed and password-protected documents show errors.
- Repository advisory locks coordinate Kagi conflict writers, not arbitrary external Git tools or all ordinary Kagi writes. Fresh preflight checks refuse detected external changes; approvals do not freeze global Git configuration, hooks or every external dependency.

## Downloads and platform notes

- **macOS Apple Silicon:** DMG or installer tar. The application is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized; Gatekeeper may require the documented first-launch steps.
- **Linux x86_64 / aarch64:** tar, AppImage zip and Debian package. Supported host system libraries are still required.
- **Windows x86_64:** zip.
- Platform-specific SHA256SUMS files accompany the packages.

For an isolated version-pinned installation, use the repository's `install.sh` with `--version v0.49.0`; `--prefix` and `--no-modify-path` are supported on macOS/Linux.
